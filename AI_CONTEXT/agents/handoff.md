# AI Agent Handoff

Before ending significant work, update `AI_CONTEXT/HANDOFF.md` with:

- date, agent, task, and objective
- completed work and changed files
- database/configuration impact
- tests and verification status
- known issues and unfinished work
- important discoveries and decisions
- the next agent's concrete next actions
- warnings and rollback information

The handoff must let an agent with no conversation history continue safely.
